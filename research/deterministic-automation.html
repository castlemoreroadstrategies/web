Deterministic Automation in Transaction Due Diligence

Leveraging local, containerized AI pipelines to accelerate property financial modeling and title diligence verification.

1. Eliminating Cognitive Bottlenecks in Underwriting

Traditional real estate underwriting and due diligence workflows rely heavily on manual document extraction and fragmented spreadsheets. By constructing deterministic, offline-capable language model pipelines with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), investment teams can ingest hundreds of pages of municipal zoning bylaws, title registries, and lease agreements in seconds.

Zero Data-Leakage Architecture

Unlike public cloud LLM endpoints, our enterprise workflows run entirely on local, sovereign compute infrastructure—guaranteeing that sensitive transaction financials and counterparty details remain strictly confidential.

2. Core Technical Components

Our internal systems architecture integrates three specialized operational layers:

  • Structured Parsing Engine: Automated OCR and parsing of complex PDF disclosures, municipal permits, and historical rent rolls directly into standardized JSON schemas.
  • Automated Sensitivity Matrices: Algorithmic cash-flow stress testing that recalculates levered IRR, DSCR, and break-even occupancy across varying interest-rate paths.
  • Covenant Verification Agents: Rule-based validation against provincial tenancy laws (e.g., Ontario Residential Tenancies Act / LTB regulations) to ensure full contractual compliance.
