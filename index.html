Operational Safeguards

Underwriting 2.0 & Governance

Mandatory Escrow & Collateral

Informed by real operational cycles, all lease-options require a minimum 4%–5% non-refundable option deposit held in escrow. This eliminates tenant default risk and fully covers maintenance reserves.

Day-1 Mortgage Underwriting

Tenant-buyers are pre-underwritten by partnered mortgage brokers on Day 1. Program participation requires mandatory quarterly credit-repair milestone tracking to guarantee exit qualification.

Legal Bifurcation & Inspections

Strict separation between standard Residential Tenancy Agreements (LTB/TAL compliant) and standalone Option contracts. Routine semi-annual property management inspections protect physical asset condition.
